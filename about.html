About me

Engineering ideas into measurable results.

My academic activity brings together electrical and electronic measurements, virtual and distributed instrumentation, electrical drives and the monitoring of energy systems. At POLITEHNICA Bucharest, I combine teaching and research with interdisciplinary projects and industry-oriented applications.

I have also worked in the coordination and management of research teams, programmes and scientific projects, alongside involvement in professional engineering organisations.
